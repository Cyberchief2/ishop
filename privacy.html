Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last updated: 1 October 2026

At iShop ("we", "our", "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our website ishop.com.ng or make a purchase from us. This policy is compliant with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and password when you create an account.
  • Order Information: Your delivery address, phone number, and product selections when you place an order.
  • Payment Information: Payment is processed by Paystack. We do not store your card details. Paystack may share your email and transaction reference with us.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, and cookies to improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Communicate order updates and delivery information
  • Manage your account and provide customer support
  • Send promotional emails (only if you opt in)
  • Prevent fraud and secure our platform
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. How We Protect Your Information

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure password hashing, and JWT-based authentication. Your data is stored on secured cloud servers.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Paystack — to process payments securely
  • Cloudinary — to host product images
  • Brevo — to send transactional emails (password reset, order confirmations)
  • Delivery partners — to deliver your order
  • Legal authorities — if required by Nigerian law

5. Your Rights (NDPR)

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@ishop.com.ng.

6. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you logged in, remember your cart, and improve the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may stop working.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a notice on our site.
