Refund & Returns Policy

Our commitment to your satisfaction

Last updated: 1 October 2026
✅ Our Promise: If you receive a damaged, defective, or wrong item, we will replace it or issue a full refund — no questions asked.

1. Return Window

You have 7 calendar days from the date of delivery to request a return. After 7 days, we cannot accept returns except for manufacturing defects.

2. Eligibility for Return (Physical Products)

To qualify for a return, the item must be:

  • Unused and in its original packaging
  • In the same condition as received
  • Accompanied by the original receipt or order reference
  • Not on our non-returnable list (see Section 5)

3. How to Request a Return

  1. Email us at info@ishop.com.ng or WhatsApp us at +234 811 480 8088
  2. Include your order reference and a photo of the item
  3. Tell us the reason for the return
  4. We will respond within 24 hours with return instructions
  5. Ship the item back to us (return shipping is on us for defective items)

4. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item:

  • We will notify you via email within 48 hours
  • Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method via Paystack
  • Refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank
  • Delivery fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error

5. Non-Returnable Items

⚠️ The following cannot be returned:
  • Digital products (e-books, software, templates) — non-refundable once downloaded
  • Personal care and beauty products that have been opened
  • Underwear, swimwear, and intimate apparel
  • Items marked "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" on the product page
  • Gift cards and vouchers

6. Damaged or Wrong Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item:

  • Report it within 48 hours of delivery
  • Provide a photo or short video showing the issue
  • We will send a free replacement OR issue a full refund — your choice
  • Return shipping is fully covered by us

7. Exchanges

We currently handle exchanges on a case-by-case basis. If you want to exchange an item (e.g., wrong size), contact us and we will arrange it. Additional shipping fees may apply for size/color exchanges.

8. Order Cancellation

  • Before shipping: Cancel free of charge by contacting us
  • After shipping: The order cannot be cancelled — you may return it once received
  • Digital products: Cannot be cancelled once purchased

9. Late or Missing Refunds

If you have not received your refund after 10 business days:

  • Check your bank account again
  • Contact your bank or card issuer — processing time may vary
  • If you still have issues, email info@ishop.com.ng with your order reference

10. Delivery Delays

If your order has not arrived within the estimated window:

  • Check the tracking link in your order confirmation email
  • Contact us and we will investigate with the courier
  • If the item is lost in transit, we will reship or refund at no cost to you
