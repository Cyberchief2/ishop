Payment Options

We offer multiple secure ways to pay for your order:

  • Debit/Credit Cards — Visa, Mastercard, Verve
  • Bank Transfer — Direct transfer from your bank
  • USSD — Quick payment via your bank's USSD code
  • Bank Account — Direct account payment via Paystack
  • OPay, PalmPay — Wallet payments supported
🔒 Secure: All payments are processed through Paystack, one of Africa's leading payment platforms. Your card details are never stored on our servers.

Cancel an Order

Changed your mind? You can cancel within a short window after ordering.

  • Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placement, provided they haven't shipped
  • Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation is no longer possible
  • To cancel, contact us immediately on WhatsApp or email help@ishop.com.ng
  • If payment was already made, a full refund will be issued within 3–14 business days

Returns & Refunds

We want you to love every purchase. If you don't, we're here to help.

Return Policy

You can return any product within 7 days from the delivery date if:

  • You received the wrong item
  • The item is damaged or defective
  • The item does not match the product description
  • You changed your mind (item must be unopened with factory seal intact)

Items That Cannot Be Returned

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following items are non-returnable:

  • Underwear, swimwear, and lingerie
  • Cosmetics and fragrances (if opened)
  • Digital products (software, e-books, courses, templates) once downloaded
  • Items damaged after delivery or used/worn

Refund Timeline

  • Approval Time: 1–3 business days after we receive and inspect your return
  • Refund Processing: 3–14 business days depending on your payment method
  • Card Payments: refunds appear on your statement as "RFND iShop"
  • Bank Transfers: credited directly to your account
  • Paystack Payments: refunded to the original payment method

Request a Return

Fill this form to start a return. We'll respond within 24 hours with next steps.

Important: For electronic items, please remove passwords and delete personal data before returning. Items with security locks cannot be processed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

How long does delivery take?
Delivery depends on your location: Lagos 1–2 days, Abuja & PH 2–4 days, other states 3–7 days, remote areas up to 10 business days.
Can I pay on delivery?
Currently, we accept online payments only via Paystack. Cash on delivery is coming soon.
Is my payment secure?
Yes. All payments are processed by Paystack using bank-grade 256-bit encryption. iShop never sees or stores your card details.
What if I receive a wrong or damaged item?
Contact us immediately on WhatsApp or fill out the return request form above. We'll arrange a free return and issue a full refund or replacement.
How do I know a product is authentic?
Every product on iShop is sourced from verified sellers and authorized distributors. We conduct quality checks before shipping.
Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
Yes — if the order hasn't shipped yet. Contact us immediately via WhatsApp to update your address.
What if I'm not available to receive the delivery?
Our delivery partner will attempt redelivery up to 3 times. If all attempts fail, the order is returned to our warehouse and we'll contact you to arrange pickup or reshipping.
Do you offer refunds for delivery fees?
If the return is due to our error (wrong, damaged, or missing item), we refund delivery fees. For change-of-mind returns, delivery fees are non-refundable.
Can I exchange a product instead of returning it?
We don't offer direct exchanges. Please return the item for a refund and place a new order for the product you want.
How do I reach customer support?
We're available 24/7 via WhatsApp (+234 811 480 8088) or email at help@ishop.com.ng. Average response time: under 1 hour.

Still need help?

Our support team is available 24/7 — we typically respond within an hour.
