1. Introduction

Welcome to iShop ("we", "us", "iShop"). By registering as a vendor on ishop.com.ng, you ("Seller", "you") agree to be bound by this Merchant Service Agreement ("Agreement").

iShop is a Nigerian online marketplace that connects sellers of physical and digital products with customers across all 36 states. This Agreement sets out the terms under which you may list, sell, and be paid for your products on iShop.

If you do not agree to these terms, do not register as a vendor.

2. Services We Provide

When you become an approved iShop vendor, we provide the following:

  • A vendor dashboard to manage products, orders, and earnings
  • Access to iShop's storefront and customer base
  • Payment processing via Paystack
  • Email notifications for orders and payouts
  • Access to our support team via WhatsApp and email

We may add, change, or remove services at any time. If we add a new paid service, we will inform you first.

3. Your Obligations as a Seller

You agree to the following:

  • List only genuine, accurate products. No counterfeit, fake, refurbished-as-new, or unauthorized products. This is non-negotiable. Violators are permanently banned.
  • Provide accurate product information. Correct name, description, price, images, and stock levels.
  • Ship within 3 business days of receiving a paid order.
  • Package products safely for delivery.
  • Comply with Nigerian law, including NCC, NAFDAC, and other regulatory requirements applicable to your category.
  • Respond to customer inquiries within 24 hours.
  • Do not use iShop to sell anything illegal, dangerous, or that infringes someone else's intellectual property.

4. Product Approval

Every product you upload is reviewed by iShop before it goes live. We may:

  • Approve the product and publish it
  • Reject it with a reason (you can fix and resubmit)
  • Remove a live product if it violates this Agreement or receives credible complaints

We aim to review submissions within 24 hours.

5. Orders, Payments, and Commission

  • Commission. iShop charges a commission on each sale, based on the product category:
    • Electronics: 10%
    • Home & Kitchen: 10%
    • Fashion: 15%
    • Beauty: 15%
    • Digital Products: 15%
    • Traditional Attire: 15%
    We may adjust commission rates with 14 days' notice via email or the vendor dashboard.
  • Payment processing. All customer payments are collected by iShop via Paystack. You do not collect payment directly from customers.
  • What you earn. For each order, you receive the product price multiplied by quantity, minus the iShop commission. Shipping fees are set by iShop and are not part of your earnings.
  • Payout schedule. Your earnings accumulate as a "pending balance." You may request a payout from your vendor dashboard at any time. Approved payout requests are processed within 3 to 5 business days to your registered bank account.
  • Refunds and returns. If a customer returns a product and receives a refund, the corresponding earnings are removed from your pending balance. You may be asked to accept the returned item.

6. Shipping and Delivery

  • You ship physical products using your preferred courier or iShop's logistics partners (when available).
  • You are responsible for the cost of shipping from your location to the customer, unless otherwise agreed.
  • iShop is not liable for shipping costs, loss, or damage during transit.
  • Returns are your responsibility unless the customer's complaint is about delivery itself.

For digital products, "shipping" means the automatic download link we email to the customer.

7. Verification Requirements

To sell on iShop and receive payouts, you must complete our verification steps:

  • Identity verification. Upload a valid government ID (NIN slip, Voter's Card, Driver's License, or Passport) and a passport-style photograph.
  • Address verification. Upload a recent utility bill (issued within the last 3 months) showing your business address.
  • Bank verification. Confirm your bank account details using our verification method.

iShop will not approve payouts to unverified bank accounts.
