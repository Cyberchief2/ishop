Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pay to become a vendor?

No. Applying and listing on iShop is completely free. We only charge a small commission when you make a sale. There are no monthly fees, no listing fees, and no setup costs.

How long does approval take?

Most applications are reviewed within 24–48 hours. You'll receive an email as soon as a decision is made. If we need more information, we'll contact you directly.

How and when do I get paid?

Payouts are processed weekly to the bank account you provide during registration. You'll see your pending balance in your vendor dashboard at all times.

What can I sell on iShop?

We accept electronics, fashion, beauty products, home & kitchen items, traditional attire, and digital products. If you're unsure whether your product fits, reach out to info@ishop.com.ng.

Who handles delivery and returns?

iShop handles logistics and customer service. You focus on packaging and getting the product to our courier partners. Returns are managed centrally — we keep you informed at every stage.

Can I sell internationally?

Currently, iShop serves customers within Nigeria only. International shipping is on our roadmap for 2026.
