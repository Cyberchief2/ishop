Our Story

Making Quality Products Accessible to Every Nigerian

ShopNaija was founded in 2024 with a simple mission: to make quality products accessible to every Nigerian, regardless of their location. We connect local and international brands with customers across all 36 states of the country.

What started as a small team of passionate entrepreneurs has grown into one of Nigeria's most trusted online marketplaces. We're proud to serve over 500,000 customers who rely on us for authentic products, fast delivery, and exceptional customer service.
